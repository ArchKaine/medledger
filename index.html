User Guide & Warnings

⚠️ Critical Warnings

Because this app respects your privacy, your data lives only on your device. There is no central server.

  • No Password Recovery: If you set an encryption password for your Data Vault and forget it, your exported backups and cloud syncs are permanently unreadable. There is no "Forgot Password" button because the developer never sees your password.
  • Browser Data: Your active schedule lives inside your browser's local storage. If you clear your browser's site data/cookies without making a backup first, your ledger will be erased. Always Backup to Device or Push to Cloud first!

📱 1. Installation & Offline Use

MedLedger is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You don't download it from an app store.

  • iOS / Safari: Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android / Chrome: Tap the Menu (⋮) at the top right and select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Once installed, the app will run entirely offline and can trigger local push notifications.

💊 2. Adding Medications

Use the "Add Medication" box at the top of the screen to build your list.

  • Type the Name and Dosage (like 10mg).
  • Times: Tap the clock box to set exactly when you take it. If a pill requires morning, noon, and night doses, click "+ Add Time" to create three separate timers for that one pill.
  • Instructions & Side Effects: Type any special rules or symptoms to watch out for. These will display permanently under the pill's name.

✅ 3. Logging Your Daily Regimen

The "Today's Regimen" list shows everything due today. It resets automatically at midnight.

  • Tap the square checkbox next to a time slot to select it.
  • Tap Log Selected to record only what you checked, or Log All to instantly record the entire day's list.
  • Forgot to log earlier? If you took a pill hours ago but forgot to tap it, use the "Time Taken" calendar box to set the actual time you took it before clicking Log. Leave it blank if you are taking it right now.

✏️ 4. Fixing Mistakes & History

Your history and routines are fully editable.

  • Edit a Pill: Tap the Pencil Icon on a medication card to change its times, instructions, side effects, or dose.
  • Delete a Pill: Tap the Pencil Icon, then tap the red Delete button to remove it from your routine forever.
  • Undo a Log: Accidentally checked something off? Navigate to the "History Log" tab and tap the Trash Can icon next to the entry. It will instantly un-check the box in today's regimen and refund the pill to your inventory.
  • Duplicate Warnings: If you accidentally double-log a scheduled pill, the History tab will flag it with a red Duplicate badge. Delete the extra entry to fix your records and refund your pill inventory.

⚙️ 5. Settings & Customization

Tap the Gear Icon at the top of the screen to access Settings.

  • Enable Local Reminders: Turn this on to let your phone send you a push notification when a pill is due.
  • Expert Mode: Turns on "Double-Clicking". You can double-tap any item on the checklist to log it instantly.
  • Enable Pill Tracker: Opt-in to track your physical pill inventory and get warnings when running low.
  • Data Management: Move old history logs to cold storage to keep the app fast. Restore them anytime you need to generate a complete report.
  • No Babysitter Mode: Turns off the "Are you sure?" pop-ups when deleting history logs.

🔐 6. Backups & The Data Vault

Your data is completely private. You hold the keys.

  • Session Unlock: Type your password once to unlock the vault for your current session. A green indicator will appear. You can now Push, Pull, or Backup multiple times without re-typing your password.
  • Locking the Vault: The vault automatically secures itself when you close the browser tab. To secure it manually before walking away, tap the Lock Now button.
  • Cloud Sync: Click "Sign in to Google" to authorize the app. Once your vault is unlocked, use the Push and Pull buttons to securely sync your encrypted data to a hidden folder in your Drive.
  • Clinician Reports: Use the export buttons to download a printable HTML file or spreadsheet of your adherence history.

📊 7. Tracking Your Progress

The History tab features built-in analytics to monitor your consistency.

  • 7-Day Adherence: A quick percentage score showing how precisely you have followed your scheduled regimen over the last week.
  • Streak Engine: Tracks your current and longest unbroken streaks of taking all scheduled medications.
  • Consistency Grid (Heatmap): A visual calendar of your habits. A Green square means you took all scheduled doses that day, Yellow means partial completion, and Gray means missed doses. Use the dropdown menu to view your 7-day, 30-day, or 90-day trends.
  • Tap-To-Inspect: Tap on any square in the heatmap calendar to view a granular breakdown of exactly what was scheduled, taken, and missed on that specific day.
  • Ghost Logs (Faded Squares): If a square is green but faded/transparent, it means you completed the day, but one or more doses were logged retroactively (using the Time Taken box to backdate them hours later). This helps distinguish real-time tracking from fuzzy memory fills.
