User Guide

💊 Adding a New Medication

Use the "Add Medication" box at the top of the screen.

  • Type in the name and the dose (like 10mg).
  • Pick how often you take it (Daily, As Needed, etc.).
  • Times: Tap the clock box to set exactly when you take it. If you take it multiple times a day, click "+ Add Time" for each dose.
  • Instructions: Type any special rules from the bottle here, like "Take with food" or "Do not crush."

✅ Checking Off Your Regimen

The "Today's Regimen" section shows everything you need to take today.

  • Tap the empty square box next to the time to select it.
  • Tap the Log Selected button to record that you took it.
  • Forgot to log earlier? Before you tap log, use the "Time Taken" calendar box to tell the system when you actually took the pill. Leave it blank if you are taking it right now.

✏️ Fixing Mistakes

Made a typo or stopped taking a medication?

  • To change a medication, tap the little Pencil Icon on its card. You can fix the times, instructions, or dose, then click Save.
  • To completely remove it, tap the Pencil Icon and then tap the red Delete button.
  • To delete a single entry from your history log, tap the Trash Can icon next to it in the Recent Logs section.

🔔 Reminders & Settings

Tap the Gear Icon at the very top of the screen.

  • Reminders: Check this box to let your device alert you when it's time to take a pill.
  • Expert Mode: Check this to allow "Double-Clicking". If you double-tap a medication, it will instantly log it without needing to press the submit button.
