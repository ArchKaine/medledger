MedLedger Manual

📝 1. Logging Medications

By default, checking a box marks it as taken at the exact moment you click "Log". The checklist groups identical items by scheduled time.

  • Batch Logging: Check multiple boxes and hit "Log Selected" to log them all at once.
  • Time Machine: If you forgot to log a pill earlier, use the date/time picker above the checklist before clicking log. This backdates the entry.

⚠️ 2. Zero-Knowledge Interaction Engine

MedLedger contains a local database of severe drug interactions. When you add a new medication, the app cross-references it against your active regimen and will explicitly warn you of known dangers (like mixing SSRIs with Tramadol, or Aspirin with Warfarin) before saving.

⚡ 3. Expert Mode

Enable Expert Mode in Settings to unlock power-user features:

  • Double-Click Logging: Double-click any unchecked item on the regimen list to instantly log it (bypassing the "Log Selected" button).
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press Ctrl + Enter to instantly submit your selected items.

📊 4. Tracking Your Progress (Analytics)

The History tab features built-in analytics to monitor your consistency.

  • 7-Day Adherence: A quick percentage score showing how precisely you have followed your scheduled regimen over the last week.
  • Streak Engine: Tracks your current and longest unbroken streaks of taking all scheduled medications.
  • Consistency Grid (Heatmap): A GitHub-style calendar of your habits. A Green square means you took all scheduled doses that day, Yellow means partial completion, and Gray means missed doses.
  • Tap-To-Inspect: Tap on any square in the heatmap to view a granular breakdown of exactly what was scheduled, taken, and missed on that specific day.
  • Ghost Logs (Faded Squares): If a square is green but faded/transparent, it means you completed the day, but one or more doses were logged retroactively (using the Time Machine box).

🔒 5. The Encrypted Vault

MedLedger stores all data locally on your device. To backup or sync your data, you must use the Vault.

  • Go to Settings and enter a Master Password.
  • Click Download Vault to encrypt your database into a secure .medvault file.
  • You can email this file to yourself and Import it on another device to seamlessly sync your history.
  • WARNING: Without the Master Password, a .medvault file is impossible to decrypt. If you lose the password, the backup is permanently locked.

☁️ 6. Google Drive Sync

You can connect MedLedger to your Google Drive to automate backups.

  • The app requests access to an invisible, protected "AppData" folder in your Drive. It cannot read your personal documents or photos.
  • Click Push to Cloud to encrypt your data with your Master Password and upload it directly to Drive.
  • On a new device, connect your Drive, enter your password, and click Pull from Cloud to instantly restore your setup.
