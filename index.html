User Guide & Warnings

⚠️ Critical Warnings

Because this app respects your privacy, your data lives only on your device. There is no central server.

  • No Password Recovery: If you set an encryption password for your Data Vault and forget it, your exported backups and cloud syncs are permanently unreadable. There is no "Forgot Password" button because the developer never sees your password.
  • Browser Data: Your active schedule lives inside your browser's local storage. If you clear your browser's site data/cookies without making a backup first, your ledger will be erased. Always Backup to Device or Push to Cloud first!

📱 1. Installation & Offline Use

MedLedger is a Progressive Web App (PWA). You don't download it from an app store.

  • iOS / Safari: Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android / Chrome: Tap the Menu (⋮) at the top right and select "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Once installed, the app runs offline natively.

💊 2. Adding Medications & Scheduling

Use the "Add Medication" box to build your list. MedLedger supports highly complex scheduling.

  • Daily / Morning / Night / Weekly: Standard schedules. Tap "+ Add Time" to create multiple specific reminders (e.g., 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM).
  • As Needed (PRN): For things like pain relievers. They always appear on your daily list, but never count against your adherence streaks if you don't take them.
  • Specific Days: Select exactly which days of the week a pill should appear (e.g., Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays). It will automatically hide itself on the off days.
  • Cyclic (On/Off): Used for medications like birth control. Set the "Days On" (e.g., 21), the "Days Off" (e.g., 7), and the date the cycle begins. The app handles the math and hides the pill during the "Off" phase.
  • Zero-Knowledge Safety: MedLedger contains a local interaction database. It will explicitly warn you if you attempt to combine dangerous medications (like mixing Warfarin and Aspirin).

✅ 3. Logging Your Daily Regimen

The "Today's Regimen" list shows everything due today. It resets automatically at midnight.

  • Tap the square checkbox next to a time slot to select it.
  • Tap Log Selected to record only what you checked, or Log All to instantly record the entire day's list.
  • Time Machine (Backdating): If you took a pill hours ago but forgot to tap it, use the "Time Taken" calendar box to set the actual time you took it before clicking Log. Leave it blank if you are taking it exactly right now.

📊 4. Tracking Your Progress (Analytics)

The History tab features clinical-grade analytics to monitor your consistency.

  • Consistency Grid (Calendar): A visual calendar of your habits.
    🟩 Green: Perfect day.
    🟨 Yellow: Partial day (missed some, took some).
    ⬜ Gray: Empty or missed day.
  • Pending vs Missed: A day stays Green if you haven't taken a pill yet, provided its scheduled time hasn't passed. Once the clock passes the target time, it becomes Missed.
  • Ghost Logs (Faded Squares): If a calendar square is faded, it means you completed the day, but one or more doses were logged retroactively (using the Time Machine box).
  • Tap-To-Inspect: Tap any square on the calendar to view a granular breakdown of exactly what was Taken, Pending, and Missed on that date.
  • Streak Engine: Tracks your current and longest unbroken streaks of taking all scheduled medications. (PRN pills and off-cycle days do not break streaks).

✏️ 5. Fixing Mistakes

  • Edit a Pill: Tap the Pencil Icon on a medication card to change its times, instructions, or cycle.
  • Undo a Log: Accidentally checked something off? Go to the "History Log" tab and tap the Trash Can icon next to the entry. It will un-check the box in today's regimen and refund the pill to your inventory.
  • Duplicate Warnings: If you accidentally double-log a scheduled pill, the History tab flags it with a red Duplicate badge.

⚙️ 6. Settings & Customization

Tap the Gear Icon to access power-user Settings.

  • Local Reminders: Let your phone send push notifications when a pill is due.
  • Expert Mode: Double-click any item on the checklist to log it instantly. Press Ctrl + Enter to batch log.
  • Pill Tracker: Opt-in to track physical pill inventory. A warning badge appears when you drop below 10 pills.
  • Data Archival: Move old history logs to cold storage to keep the app lightning fast. Restore them anytime you need to generate a complete report.

🔐 7. The Encrypted Vault & Sync

Your data is completely private. You hold the encryption keys.

  • Session Unlock: Type your Master Password once to unlock the vault for your session. A green indicator will appear.
  • Backup Device: Encrypts your database into a secure .medvault file to store on a USB drive or email to yourself. Use "Restore Device" to load it on a new phone.
  • Google Drive Sync: Click "Sign in to Google." Use Push/Pull to securely sync your encrypted .medvault file directly to an invisible, protected AppData folder in your Google Drive.
