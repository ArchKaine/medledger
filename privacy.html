Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 3, 2026

1. Data Collection and Storage

MedLedger is a strictly local-first application. All data you enter into the application (including medication names, schedules, dosages, and historical logs) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (IndexedDB).

The developer collects absolutely zero personal data, telemetry, or analytics. We do not operate central databases, and we have no physical or technical ability to view, access, transmit, or sell your health data.

2. Google Drive Integration (drive.appdata)

If you choose to enable the optional Cloud Sync feature, MedLedger requests access to a restricted, hidden folder in your personal Google Drive via the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.

  • What we do with it: The app uses this folder strictly to upload and download an AES-GCM encrypted backup of your local database, allowing you to sync data across your own devices.
  • What we DO NOT do: The app does not read, modify, or interact with any of your personal files, photos, or documents in your broader Google Drive.
  • Developer Access: The developer of MedLedger has no access to your Google Drive account, your backups, or your encryption passwords.

3. Encryption and Security

Before any data is synced to your Google Drive or exported from your device, it is encrypted locally on your hardware using an AES-GCM algorithm and a password only you know. If you lose or forget this password, your data cannot be recovered by the developer or by Google. You hold the exclusive keys to your records.

4. Cookies and Tracking

MedLedger does not use tracking cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising scripts. We use standard browser local storage solely to remember your app configurations (such as Dark Mode preferences, interface toggles, and notification timestamps).
