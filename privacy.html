Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

1. Core Philosophy: Zero-Knowledge & Local-First

MedLedger is engineered strictly as a local-first application. The developers of MedLedger collect absolutely zero personal data, medical information, telemetry, or behavioral analytics. We do not operate a central database, and we have no physical or technical capability to view, access, transmit, or monetize your health data.

All information you enter into the application—including medication names, schedules, dosages, side effects, and historical logs—is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage (IndexedDB).

2. Data Encryption and The Vault

To facilitate secure backups, MedLedger features a "Data Vault." When you set a Master Password, your database is encrypted locally on your hardware using industry-standard AES-GCM cryptographic algorithms before it ever leaves the application context.

  • The encryption key is derived solely from your password and is never transmitted to us.
  • There is no password recovery mechanism. Because we do not store your password, we cannot reset it. If you lose your password, any exported backups or synced files will be permanently irretrievable.

3. Google Drive Integration (drive.appdata)

If you explicitly opt-in to the Cloud Sync feature, MedLedger will request authentication via Google Identity Services and ask for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission scope.

  • Strict Isolation: This permission scope strictly limits the application to a single, invisible application-specific folder within your Google Drive.
  • What we do with it: The app only uses this folder to upload and download your AES-GCM encrypted .medvault backup files, allowing you to synchronize your own data across your personal devices.
  • What we DO NOT do: MedLedger cannot see, read, modify, or interact with any of your personal files, documents, or photos in your broader Google Drive account.

4. Clinical Data & Interaction Engine

MedLedger utilizes two separate systems to provide clinical information, both designed to protect your privacy:

  • Zero-Knowledge Interactions: When you add a medication, the App cross-references it against a local database of known drug interactions. This reference file is bundled with the application and queried entirely offline on your device. Your medication list is never sent to an external server to perform this check.
  • Third-Party Clinical Lookups: If enabled in your settings, typing a medication name prompts the app to query public databases (OpenFDA/Wikidata) for basic drug descriptions. These queries are one-way requests containing only the generic text string of the medication name. They do not transmit your history, inventory, adherence scores, or identity.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

MedLedger does not utilize tracking cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising frameworks. The application relies entirely on HTML5 Local Storage and IndexedDB to maintain the core functionality of the application. This includes saving your user interface preferences (such as Dark Mode), configuration toggles, and notification timestamps.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

Because your data is owned and controlled entirely by you on your local device, exercising your right to deletion is absolute and immediate. To permanently destroy your MedLedger data, you only need to clear your browser's site data/cache for this application or uninstall the Progressive Web App (PWA) from your device. Once cleared locally, the unencrypted data ceases to exist.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in application features or regulatory requirements. Because we have no central database of users, we cannot email you regarding updates. Changes will be posted to this page, and your continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of those changes.
